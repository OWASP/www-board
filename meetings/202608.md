#### Motion to delay the October public Board meeting to AppSec San Francisco 2026

**Background** The public Board meeting in October is currently scheduled for the 27th of October 2026. The public Board meeting should be moved to be in person at AppSec San Francisco 2026, on Wednesday November 4, 2026, at a time and meeting room to be determined by meeting room availability. This will allow the Board to meet in person with the community and to allow the Board to attend the conference. Passing this motion will also move the Board prep call one week later.

Motion: "Resolved, that the OWASP Foundation Board of Directors moves the October 2026 public Board meeting to be held in person at AppSec San Francisco 2026 on November 4, 2026, with the exact time and meeting location to be published on the OWASP website and calendar no later than one week prior to the meeting."

Sponsor: Ricardo Griffith
Second: Sam Stepanyan

#### Trip report for Black Hat and DEFCON 34

**Background** An update from Stacey and Starr on the trip to Black Hat and DEFCON 34, including any relevant information for the Board.

#### Discussion on project onboarding as it relates to sanctioned countries

**Background** The OWASP Foundation may have a project that is led by leaders who are in a sanctioned OFAC country. The Executive Director has reached out for specialist legal advice on if we do have project leaders who are from Iran what implications and minimum due diligence we need to do to ensure we are not violating any OFAC sanctions.

The Board will discuss the legal advice and determine if any action is needed.

#### Discussion on status of policy reviews

**Background** A review of the current state of policy reviews and the next steps for the Board to take to ensure that all policies are reviewed and updated as needed. Invited guest Avi Douglen will assist with this update.

#### Discussion on Chapters tickets status

**Background** A review of the current state of chapter tickets from Harold Blankenship.

#### Discussion on project and chapter committee status

**Background** The project committee meets rarely and is dormant. The chapter committee needs one more officer to be fully staffed. The Board will discuss the current state of these committees and determine if any action is needed.
